# Research

Our group does theoretical research on dynamic phenomena in quantum-mechanical systems at the nanoscale, with a particular focus on transfer and transport of quantum information. We often us e a combination of analytical and numerical techniques. Examples of research topics we are interested in are: 

**Distribution and routing of quantum information** - Understanding how quantum information (in particular entanglement) is distributed in interacting quantum systems and how this process can be used to steer and control flow of quantum information still has many open aspects. We are interested in problems related to this, such as designing and analysing optimal quantum routing operations in solid-state hardware [link Arnau] and predicting the distribution of quantum information by means of out-of-time-ordered correlators [preprint in progress]

**Quantum state transfer** -  We are also nterested in transport of quantum states through quantum devices that can serve as quantum data buses. One example is the development of (super)adiabatic protocols for achieving quantum state transfer with, in principle, very high accuracy and minimal control [link Rodrigo].
